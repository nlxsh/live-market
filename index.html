    Inside the engine

    A custom scoring model reads real 1-minute candles and live ticks, detects the market regime, then maps the balance of evidence to a next-candle direction and a confidence score.

    EMA 9 / 21 / 50

    Fast and slow EMAs set the short-term trend, while the 50 EMA filters against false starts. Alignment across all three stacks bullish or bearish weight.

    Trend

    MACD 12 · 26 · 9

    The MACD histogram reveals momentum shifts and confirms trend direction. An expanding histogram multiplies the directional score.

    Momentum

    RSI 14

    Wilder-smoothed RSI gauges overbought and oversold pressure. Readings above 55 support upside; below 45 support downside.

    Strength

    Stochastic RSI

    Stochastic applied to RSI amplifies exhaustion signals. Extremes above 80 or below 20 trigger mean-reversion adjustments.

    Reversal

    Bollinger 20 · 2

    Price position within the bands reveals momentum in the middle and mean-reversion opportunities near the outer bands.

    Volatility

    ADX regime proxy

    A simplified ADX detects whether the market is trending, ranging, or mixed. Weights shift accordingly — trend weights dominate in trends.

    Regime

    Candle patterns

    Engulfing, hammer, shooting star, doji, and strong-body closes all contribute calibrated signals to the directional score.

    Price action

    Tick volume

    Forex feeds return zero contract volume, so the engine uses tick volume. Above-average activity amplifies the existing directional score.
