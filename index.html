    Inside the engine

    A focused scoring model. Each signal reads the last 14 closed 1-minute candles plus the running candle, using classic indicators and comprehensive candlestick-pattern detection as confirmation.

    EMA 9 / 21

    Fast and slow exponential moving averages define the short-term trend. The gap between them, normalized by ATR, drives the trend label and a large share of the score.

    Trend

    SMA 20

    The simple moving average acts as a baseline bias. Price above the SMA supports upside; below supports downside. Distance is scaled by recent volatility.

    Bias

    RSI 14

    Wilder-smoothed relative strength index gauges momentum. Above 55 supports upside, below 45 supports downside. Neutral readings contribute lightly.

    Momentum

    Bollinger 20 · 2

    Price position within the bands reveals pressure. Upper half supports upside, lower half downside. Extreme touches dampen the signal to reduce chasing.

    Volatility

    30 Candlestick Patterns

    Detects engulfing, stars, harami, dojis, hammers, and multi-candle formations to confirm the direction. Acts purely as a confirmation filter.

    Confirmation

    14-bar Window

    The engine looks at the last 14 closed candles plus the running candle. Standard periods are warmed up on the fuller history so the read stays stable and clean.
